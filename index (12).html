Our story

Every cup is a home run.

Blue Brew is a hometown coffee brand with a ballpark heart. Our three signature blends are roasted fresh in small batches with our partners at Lucky's in Claremont. Every drink gets pulled with the same care, whether you visit our baseball-themed shop or book the cart for your event.

Before the first pitch

Questions? We've got answers.

How much space does the cart need?

A flat, level area roughly 8 by 8 feet works well, with room for a line to form. Let us know your space and we'll confirm it fits.

Do you need power or water?

Access to a standard outlet nearby is ideal. Tell us about your venue when you book and we'll work out the details.

Can you set up outdoors?

Yes. Patios, parks, parking lots and backyards are all fair game. For hot or windy days, some shade is a plus.

What areas do you serve?

Our home base is 1945 E Riverside Dr, Suite 3A, in Ontario, right next door to Ontario Coin Laundromat. We serve the Inland Empire and nearby areas. Ask about events farther out.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekends fill up fast, so booking 3 to 4 weeks ahead is best. Reach out anyway if your date is sooner. We'll do our best to fit you in.
